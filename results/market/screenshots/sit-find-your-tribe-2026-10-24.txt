Find Your Tribe

The Office of SITizen Experience is committed to shaping students into competent, compassionate changemakers and leaders who embodies the SITizen-DNA. We offer diverse platforms such as leadership development, varsity sports, performing arts, special interests initiatives and wellness programmes. With over 170 student organisations spanning various interests, we cultivate a vibrant, safe, and inclusive campus community.

 

Community Service, SITizen Ambassadors, Special Interest, Student Industry Chapters & Wellness

Student Clubs provide a platform for students to cultivate holistic skill sets, develop leadership capabilities, and prioritize wellness, extending beyond the boundaries of the academic curriculum. Simultaneously, Student Industry Chapters play a vital role in enhancing students' professional development by offering opportunities to network with industry professionals. 

Community Service Groups serve to make a positive impact through volunteer opportunities including the mentoring and befriending of youths, integration of migrant brothers, as well as interacting with and guiding Persons with Disabilities (PwDs) and the elderly. These opportunities will allow students to gain valuable experiences, make new friends, and help create a more compassionate community within SIT. 

SITizen Ambassadors are a dedicated group who personally exemplify the SITizen-DNA. As strategic partners and university spokespersons, they champion SIT's vision at key external platforms, actively participate in university initiatives, and help shape SIT’s future by delivering major projects. 

Special Interest Clubs span across multiple genres, including community service, language, food and culture appreciation, sustainability, and eSports, allowing students to explore their passions and connect with like-minded individuals. 

Student Industry Chapters cater to various sectors, such as engineering, conventions, travel, and audit industries, enabling students to gain industry insights and establish valuable connections. 

Wellness Groups foster a supportive and inclusive campus community through student-led initiatives that promote mental well-being, diversity, inclusion, and peer support. Guided by OSE wellness staff, student leaders create safe spaces for students to connect, build resilience, and cultivate a strong sense of belonging across the SIT community.

Performing Arts

SIT Performing Arts groups allow students to develop their talent and passion in music, dance, theatre and instrumental. Student artistes are often invited to perform at various internal gigs and external events at national level. The annual highlight is a diverse and remarkable production, ExpresSIT, showcasing perfomances from these Performing Arts groups. Introductory workshops and activities are organised by students for students to foster a vibrant campus and enhances SITizens' self-esteem and overall well-being through Performing Arts, while gaining valuable performing experience and opportunities.

Special Projects 

Special Projects provide students with the opportunity to lead the planning and delivery of signature university-wide initiatives that shape the SIT experience. As members of these organising committees, students develop leadership, project management, and collaboration skills while creating meaningful experiences that strengthen the SIT community and cultivate a strong sense of SITizen identity. 

Arts Festival (art;SITry) is SIT’s signature student-led annual arts festival that celebrates creativity, diversity, and community spirit. The festival aims to strengthen campus-community connections within the Punggol Digital District, drive holistic student development through leadership, and celebrate SITizen talents while fostering a vibrant and inclusive campus culture. 

First Year Experience (FYE) plays a pivotal role in welcoming new students into the SIT community. Students organise a series of orientation and transition programmes, including Helix to build cross-cluster connections and engage with the community; Day Zero which introduces freshmen to their academic programmes and support network; and Club Fair, where more than 170 student organisations inspire students to explore opportunities beyond the classroom and enrich their university experience. 

Inter-Cluster Games (ICG) is SIT’s annual flagship sporting event that brings SITizens and staff together through sports. The games foster camaraderie, teamwork, and sportsmanship across all clusters, while strengthening bonds across the SIT community. 

SITizen Yearbook is a curation of the people, experiences, and milestones that shape the unique journeys of the graduating cohort. More than a collection of photographs, the yearbook serves as a timeless keepsake that celebrates friendships, personal growth, and the SITizen journey.

Sports Clubs & Varsity Teams

SIT Football Men’s Varsity Team emerged as Champions in the Singapore University Games 2023 (SUniG). This is the first time SIT has won a gold for football at SUniG.

Student Clubs provide an avenue for students to cultivate holistic skill sets and pursue their personal interests, extending beyond the confines of the academic curriculum. Engaging in Sports and Martial Arts offers a distinctive blend of discipline, tenacity, and grace, fostering not only physical well-being but also mental and emotional fitness. By participating in these activities, students can embrace a balanced lifestyle, nurturing both mind and body, while acquiring valuable life skills that go beyond the classroom.

Student Management Committees

Student Management Committees (SMCs) serve as the representative voice of their respective programme cohorts, looking after the welfare of their peers. Acting as a vital bridge between the student body and the university, SMCs strive to enhance the overall student learning experience. Additionally, these committees proactively organise regular activities that foster strong bonds among students, both within and beyond their academic programmes.
