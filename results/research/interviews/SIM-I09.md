## A. Background and concrete episode
A departmental training programme already uses an institutional registration system, but waitlisting is handled by email and exporting a participant list requires approval.

## B. Current workflow
Exports from the institutional system, creates supporting sheets per training session, contacts waitlisted people and updates the institutional records afterwards.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
Clarify data retention, permissions, export and institutional approval before procurement can be considered.
