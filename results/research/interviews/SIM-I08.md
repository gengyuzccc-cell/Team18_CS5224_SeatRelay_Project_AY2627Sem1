# SIM-I08 interview record

> Data source: an authored interview scenario. See Section 2 of the research report for the generation method.

- Profile: Student coordinator of a career-development programme whose small team can choose free tools
- Role: organiser
- Consent: not applicable to generated scenarios

## A. Background and concrete episode
An evening session is cancelled at short notice. The team must ask every participant about alternative times because the form did not collect preferences.

## B. Current workflow
Downloads registrations, filters confirmed participants, emails them about alternatives and manually assembles waitlist and attendance lists.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
Collect acceptable times at registration and reallocate after a session cancellation while keeping a clear history.

## D. Counterexamples and limitations
An automatically issued offer does not establish attendance; confirmed participants must not be reported as actual attendees.

## E. Quantitative assumptions

Annual events: 18; multi-session events: 6; baseline coordination: 3.0 hours/event. Participant and organiser effort refer to their respective protocol roles.
Assumed too-cheap / bargain / expensive / too-expensive prices: [0, 5, 10, 20] SGD; unit: per organisation per month. These are not purchasing commitments.

## F. Scripted statements
- `SIM-I08-Q1` (scripted statement, not a transcript): The form only asked which day they wanted; after cancellation we realised we did not know their alternatives.
- `SIM-I08-Q2` (scripted statement, not a transcript): Receiving a backfill offer and actually attending are two different things.
