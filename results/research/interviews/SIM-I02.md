# SIM-I02 interview record

> Data source: an authored interview scenario. See Section 2 of the research report for the generation method.

- Profile: First-year student using a waitlist for the first time
- Role: participant
- Consent: not applicable to generated scenarios

## A. Background and concrete episode
Submits a waitlist form for a full session but interprets the response receipt as successful registration, only asking the organiser the day before the event.

## B. Current workflow
Saves a screenshot after submitting and searches email for confirmation; does not clearly distinguish receipt of a request from a confirmed seat.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
State plainly whether a seat is available and whether an acceptance action is required to keep it.

## D. Counterexamples and limitations
A countdown may increase anxiety; first-time users need to know whether they can remain queued after expiry.

## E. Quantitative assumptions

Annual events: 8; multi-session events: 2; baseline coordination: 0.5 hours/event. Participant and organiser effort refer to their respective protocol roles.
Assumed too-cheap / bargain / expensive / too-expensive prices: [0, 0, 1, 3] SGD; unit: per event. These are not purchasing commitments.

- `SIM-I02-Q2` (scripted statement, not a transcript): What does held mean, and how is it different from confirmed?
