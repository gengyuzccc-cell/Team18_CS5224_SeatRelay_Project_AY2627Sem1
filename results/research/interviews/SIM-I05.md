# SIM-I05 interview record

> Data source: an authored interview scenario. See Section 2 of the research report for the generation method.

- Profile: Fourth-year student attending career workshops who is concerned about fairness
- Role: participant
- Consent: not applicable to generated scenarios

## A. Background and concrete episode
A group chat shows that a later registrant received a Thursday seat first. The student accepts only Tuesday but mistakenly believes someone has jumped the queue.

## B. Current workflow
Checks group messages and spreadsheet timestamps, then privately asks the organiser about queue order.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
Explain that a later registrant who accepts another session may receive a seat first.

## D. Counterexamples and limitations
One queue number alone cannot explain allocation across sessions; the head of the queue is not guaranteed the next seat in any session.

## E. Quantitative assumptions

Annual events: 16; multi-session events: 6; baseline coordination: 0.4 hours/event. Participant and organiser effort refer to their respective protocol roles.
Assumed too-cheap / bargain / expensive / too-expensive prices: [0, 1, 2, 5] SGD; unit: per event. These are not purchasing commitments.
