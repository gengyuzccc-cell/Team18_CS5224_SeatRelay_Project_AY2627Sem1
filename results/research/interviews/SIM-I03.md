# SIM-I03 interview record

> Data source: an authored interview scenario. See Section 2 of the research report for the generation method.

- Profile: Third-year student on an internship who rarely checks personal email during work
- Role: participant
- Consent: not applicable to generated scenarios

## A. Background and concrete episode
Receives a backfill email during an internship and sees it at lunch, after the seat has moved to the next person.

## B. Current workflow
Disables notifications at work and checks email at lunch rather than keeping the registration page open.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
Know the confirmation window before joining, so that the student can choose events whose response requirements are practical.

## D. Counterexamples and limitations
Fifteen minutes is not universally suitable; automation can repeatedly exclude people unable to respond quickly.

## E. Quantitative assumptions

Annual events: 10; multi-session events: 3; baseline coordination: 0.2 hours/event. Participant and organiser effort refer to their respective protocol roles.
Assumed too-cheap / bargain / expensive / too-expensive prices: [0, 1, 3, 5] SGD; unit: per event. These are not purchasing commitments.
