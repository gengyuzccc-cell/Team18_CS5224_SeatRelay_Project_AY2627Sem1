# SIM-I01 interview record

> Data source: an authored interview scenario. See Section 2 of the research report for the generation method.

- Profile: Second-year student with a changing timetable, familiar with forms and group chats
- Role: participant
- Consent: not applicable to generated scenarios

## A. Background and concrete episode
The same CV workshop runs on Tuesday and Thursday. Concerned about a timetable change, the student registers for both and later cannot find how to cancel one.

## B. Current workflow
Opens two forms from a group message, submits both, keeps two confirmation emails and contacts the organiser once the class timetable is settled.

## C. Needs and trade-offs
Select both acceptable sessions while occupying only one seat; provide cancellation without searching old emails.

## D. Counterexamples and limitations
If neither selected session remains suitable, the student must withdraw and register again rather than receive an unselected session.

## E. Quantitative assumptions

Annual events: 12; multi-session events: 4; baseline coordination: 0.3 hours/event. Participant and organiser effort refer to their respective protocol roles.
Assumed too-cheap / bargain / expensive / too-expensive prices: [0, 0, 2, 4] SGD; unit: per event. These are not purchasing commitments.

## F. Scripted statements
- `SIM-I01-Q1` (scripted statement, not a transcript): I register twice to keep my options, not because I want to attend twice.
- `SIM-I01-Q2` (scripted statement, not a transcript): A place where I can cancel is more useful than another notification.
