\section*{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Market:} organisation counts are official lower bounds; NUS, SMU and SIT figures were read
  from search excerpts of the official pages because direct download was blocked
  (\pending{dated browser screenshots of these three pages}); $p$, $s$ and the value per event come
  only from the organiser interviews.
\item \emph{Cost:} usage per registration is modelled (\S\ref{sec:business}) until measured usage is
  priced; X-Ray is priced at 100\,\% sampling (an upper bound); operations labour and the on-prem
  machine's capacity are assumptions\IfPending{\PerfLocalCpuPeakPct}{ (the on-prem stack has not been load-tested)}{};
  availability is not equalised between the two builds.
\item \emph{Experiments:} the \code{dev} stage, a 60\,s hold for expiry experiments and synthetic
  accounts; the mechanism comparison uses an assumed behaviour model and ideal timers.
\item \emph{Operations:} one AWS region; SES sandbox until production access is granted (in-app
  notifications for unverified addresses).
\end{itemize}
