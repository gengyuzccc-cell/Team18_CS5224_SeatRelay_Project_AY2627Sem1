\section*{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Market:} organisation counts are official lower bounds; the NUS and SIT figures were
  confirmed on their pages with dated browser screenshots on 24 Oct 2026 (SIT's had risen from 150 to
  170), and SMU's on its current campus-life page, as its 2022 page is no longer public
  (\code{results/market/screenshots/}); $p$, $s$ and the value per event come only from the organiser
  interviews.
\item \emph{Cost:} usage per registration is modelled (\S\ref{sec:business}), as CloudWatch does
  not count registrations; X-Ray is priced at 100\,\% sampling (an upper bound); operations labour
  and the on-prem machine's capacity are assumed\IfPending{\PerfLocalCpuPeakPct}{ (the on-prem stack has not been load-tested)}{};
  availability is not equalised.
\item \emph{Experiments:} \code{dev} stage, synthetic accounts, 60\,s expiry holds; the replay

  day), but the sender is a gmail.com address, so in-app notifications stay the primary channel, and
  Cognito's sign-up codes are capped at 50 a day.
\end{itemize}
