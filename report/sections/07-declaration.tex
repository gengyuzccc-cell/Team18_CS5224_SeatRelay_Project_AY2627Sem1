\section*{Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Market:} organisation counts are lower bounds (official figures; NTU counted by us); NUS,
  SMU and SIT were re-checked with dated browser screenshots on 24 Oct 2026
  (\code{results/market/screenshots/}; SIT's rose from 150 to 170, SMU's is from its campus-life page
  as the 2022 page is gone); this prototype edition uses scripted assumptions for $p$, $s$,
  coordination effort and price reactions. The price (\sd10 a month) and adoption share $a$ are proposals,
  not validated demand. Research provenance is retained in the dataset metadata.
\item \emph{Cost:} usage per registration is modelled (\S\ref{sec:business}), as CloudWatch does
  not count registrations; X-Ray is priced at 100\,\% sampling (an upper bound); operations labour
  and the on-prem machine's capacity are assumed\IfPending{\PerfLocalCpuPeakPct}{ (the on-prem stack has not been load-tested)}{};
  availability is not equalised.
\item \emph{Experiments:} \code{dev} stage, synthetic accounts, 60\,s expiry holds; the replay
  assumes a behaviour model and ideal timers.
\item \emph{Operations:} one AWS region; SES production access granted (50{,}000 e-mails a day) but
  from a gmail.com sender, so in-app notifications stay primary; Cognito sends at most 50 sign-up codes a day.
\end{itemize}
