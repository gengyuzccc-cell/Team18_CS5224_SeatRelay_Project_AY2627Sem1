SeatRelay

One waitlist for every session of a workshop.

Register once and rank the sessions you can attend. A freed seat is held for the next person in line who can attend that session, not left empty or waiting for an organiser’s e-mail.

How it works

  1. Tick every session you could attend and rank them.
  2. Wait in one queue. When a seat you can take frees up and you are first, it is held for you (15 min by default).

    Staged run with test accounts and 1-minute holds.

  3. Accept to keep it. Decline or let it expire: only that session is dropped, and the seat is held for the next person at once.
  4. Organisers see every hand-over and its delay.

Serverless on AWS

  • One lane per workshop, decided in arrival order; a version check catches any overlap.
  • One transaction: seats, queue, offer and notice change together.
  • One timer per hold: a late timer cannot touch a newer offer.

Cost follows use: US$13.30 → US$5.91 per 1,000 registrations from 1k to 100k a month (list prices, our cost model); self-hosting is cheaper above about 57,000.
