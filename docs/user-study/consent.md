# Consent form — SeatRelay user evaluation

Please read this page. Ask anything that is unclear. Sign only if you agree.

## What this is about

We are comparing two ways of signing up for a workshop that runs several identical sessions: a tool our team built (**SeatRelay**) and the common current practice (**one Google Form per session plus a spreadsheet**). We want to know which one is faster, which one causes fewer mistakes, and how people feel about using each.

## What you will do (about 40 minutes)

1. Answer 5 short background questions (e.g. whether you have organised events before).
2. Use the first tool to do 4–5 small tasks with made-up workshop data (e.g. sign up, read your waitlist position, confirm or cancel a seat; or, if you have organiser experience, create a workshop and handle a cancellation).
3. Fill in a 10-question questionnaire about that tool.
4. Do the same with the second tool, with different made-up data, and fill in the questionnaire again.
5. Answer 4 short interview questions.

We are testing the tools, not you. Some tasks may be confusing on purpose — that is exactly what we want to learn.

## What we record

| Recorded | Not recorded |

| Whether each task was completed, how long it took, how many wrong clicks and requests for help, your questionnaire answers, notes of what you said | Your name, e-mail or student number in the data (the consent form is kept separately on paper) |

Data are stored as plain CSV/Markdown files in the team's project repository under your anonymous ID only. Summary statistics and anonymised quotes (e.g. "P07 said the countdown was clear") may appear in our course report and presentation. The link between your name and your ID exists only on this paper form, which is kept by the team until the course grade is released and then destroyed.

## Your rights

- Participation is **voluntary**. There is no payment and no course credit; declining has no consequence.
- You may **stop at any time** without giving a reason, and you may skip any task or question.
- You may **withdraw your data** up to 2026-11-10 by e-mailing the facilitator with your participant ID; we then delete your rows.
- Workshop names, dates and the other "participants" you will see are fictitious. Nothing you do affects any real event.
