## Three questions to prepare

1. **What does this research demonstrate?** Nine interview scenarios and a formal study with twelve
   participants demonstrate the prototype evaluation workflow. The methodology identifies the data as simulated.
2. **Why does the study miss targets?** The declared scenario preserves held-versus-confirmed errors,
   ordering/date-entry help and negative reactions. Completion separates unassisted and assisted attempts;
   generated outcomes are not tuned to pass every reference line.
3. **How are the calculations checked?** SUS is recomputed from all ten items. Task time is summed per
   participant before pairing. The generation check reproduces source files and report macros,
   while the manifest records assumptions and hashes. Existing system experiments remain separate.
